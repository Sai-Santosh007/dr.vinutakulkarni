About Dr. Vinuta Kulkarni

Eye Specialist & Assistant Professor MBBS, MS, FPRS

Dr. Vinuta Kulkarni is an accomplished eye specialist with more than 10 years of clinical and surgical experience in ophthalmology. She has treated over 5,000 patients, offering precision-oriented diagnostic assessments and personalized surgical solutions.

Currently serving as an Assistant Professor at Kempegowda Institute of Medical Sciences (KIMS), Bangalore, Dr. Kulkarni actively shapes the future of ophthalmology. She is an enthusiastic mentor involved in the surgical training of budding ophthalmologists as well as academic instruction for undergraduate and postgraduate medical students.

Her medical practice is rooted in ethical care, deep clinical knowledge, and an unwavering commitment to patient safety and long-term vision health.

✓ Academic Faculty: Assistant Professor at Kempegowda Institute of Medical Sciences (KIMS), Bangalore
✓ Fellowship Trained: Specialized training in Phaco Refractive Surgery (FPRS)

Areas of Clinical Expertise

Focused, high-standard ophthalmic care delivering diagnostic accuracy and surgical precision across four core disciplines.

Comprehensive Eye Care

Thorough diagnostic assessments to evaluate total eye wellness, detect conditions early, and safeguard visual acuity.

  • Complete evaluation of eye health
  • Refraction and glasses prescription
  • Glaucoma evaluation & monitoring
  • Retina evaluation

Cataract Surgeries

Advanced surgical solutions designed to safely restore crisp clarity and enhance visual quality of life.

  • Different types of cataract surgeries
  • Premium intraocular lens (IOL) implantation
  • Precision-focused surgical care
  • Pre- and post-operative surgical management

Outpatient Eye Care

Accessible, attentive clinical care addressing routine and acute ocular discomfort and disorders.

  • Outpatient department (OPD) services
  • Diagnosis of eye-related diseases
  • Ocular surface & allergy treatment
  • Dry eye and ocular strain management

Refractive Surgeries

Detailed pre-procedure assessment and customized planning for individuals seeking visual freedom.

  • Evaluation for refractive surgery candidacy
  • Planning for reducing dependence on glasses
  • Customized corneal and ocular analysis
  • Patient counseling on expected visual outcomes
